Complete English Tenses Guide

Present Tense

  • Simple Present: I study English daily.
  • Present Continuous: I am studying grammar now.
  • Present Perfect: I have completed the lesson.
  • Present Perfect Continuous: I have been practicing for 2 hours.

Past Tense

  • Simple Past: I studied grammar yesterday.
  • Past Continuous: I was reading a book.
  • Past Perfect: I had finished before he arrived.
  • Past Perfect Continuous: I had been waiting.

Future Tense

  • Simple Future: I will learn English.
  • Future Continuous: I will be practicing tomorrow.
  • Future Perfect: I will have completed the course.
  • Future Perfect Continuous: I will have been learning.

Advanced Grammar & Sentence Building

  • Parts of Speech: Nouns, Pronouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs, Prepositions, Conjunctions, Interjections.
  • Active & Passive Voice: Active: "Saeed teaches English." | Passive: "English is taught by Saeed."
  • Direct & Indirect Speech: Direct: He said, "I am learning." | Indirect: He said that he was learning.
